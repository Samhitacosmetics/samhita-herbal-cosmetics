SAMHITA HERBAL COSMETICS WEBSITE

HOW TO VIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. Navigate using the top menu. The site is a static multi-page website and does not require a server for its core pages.

PAGES
- index.html: Home
- shop.html: Product collection with category filters
- product.html: Product detail page (opened from product cards)
- about.html: Brand story
- ingredients.html: Brand philosophy
- contact.html: Contact information and email enquiry form

CUSTOMISE BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Confirm the company address, telephone number, email address and all product details. The phone number and address are transcribed from the supplied product images.
- The contact form opens WhatsApp using the contact number shown on the supplied product labels. Confirm this number before publishing.
- Add verified social media links, product prices, shipping details, privacy policy and terms before accepting online orders.
- Product ingredient summaries are introductory only. Use the approved complete ingredient declarations and directions from each product label for final publication.

TECHNOLOGY
HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript. Product photos and logo supplied by the user are included in assets/. Google Fonts are loaded online; system fallback fonts are provided.
